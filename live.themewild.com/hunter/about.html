A bird sanctuary, where the client chooses how he wants to start his tour, getting an interactive talk about the animal, with benefits such as stickers or pins that will give him a discount in our souvenir stores.

    We seek to protect, rehabilitate and relocate birds in Panama through rescue, education and strategic alliances.
