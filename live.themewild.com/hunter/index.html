    We are a bird sanctuary Biowild, rescuing birds.

    Protecting birds means more than simply keeping them safe from immediate harm. It involves preserving their habitats, ensuring access to food and clean water, reducing threats caused by human activities, and creating environments where they can live, reproduce, and migrate safely.

    Services

    What We Offers To Panama

    Discover our country as a safe and beautiful home for many bird species, with diverse habitats and rich biodiversity.

    Bird Identification

    BioWild provides an intelligent bird-identification tool that allows users to identify birds by uploading a photo, uploading an audio recording, or selecting visible characteristics.

    Read More

    Interactive Bird Guide

    Users can explore a catalog of Panamanian bird species and use an interactive map to discover where different species can be found within the sanctuary.

    Read More

    Birdwatching & Guided Tours

    BioWild offers guided birdwatching tours and nature experiences where visitors can learn about Panama's biodiversity while observing birds in their natural or protected environment.

    Read More

    Environmental Education

    BioWild provides educational experiences through quizzes, challenges, badges, interactive species information, workshops, and conservation content.

    Read More

    Conservation Monitoring

    Users can report bird sightings by recording the species, date, time, location, and optionally uploading a photograph.

    Read More

    Reservations & Membership

    BioWild offers visitors a digital platform to check visiting hours, purchase tickets, reserve guided tours, participate in special events, and access memberships.
